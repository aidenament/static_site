Expected Value of A Random Point in a Square to its Nearest Edge

< Back to Projects

One Closed Form for Every Dimension

Drop a point at random inside a unit square. How far is it from the nearest edge? The paper does the square, then the cube, then every dimension at once, and closes with what the answer says about how much of a high-dimensional cube sits near its boundary.

The problem came from my Math 170A final. I worked out the general case afterward, in July 2023.
