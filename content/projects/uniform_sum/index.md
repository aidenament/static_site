## The Expected Number of Uniform Variables Required to Exceed 1

Pick numbers uniformly at random between 0 and 1 and add them up one at a time. How many do you need, on average, before the total passes 1? The answer is e, and there is no exponential anywhere in the setup.

An interviewer asked me this and I didn't get it. I wrote it up afterward, in August 2024.
